About Live Ethio

Live Ethio is Ethiopia’s leading real estate marketplace and full-service agency, based in Addis Ababa. We provide a trusted platform for anyone looking to buy, rent, sell, or manage property in Ethiopia. Our listings are verified, our service is professional, and our team includes expert in-house real estate agents who understand both local and international client needs.

Whether you're interested in apartments, houses, condominiums, office space, warehouses, or investment land, we deliver complete, transparent support from search to closing. Through our real estate website and dedicated agent team, we help individuals, families, landlords, diaspora, and foreign investors confidently navigate the Ethiopian property market.
